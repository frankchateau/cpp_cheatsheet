# Casting

## Cast keywords

### const_cast

Add/remove `const`, `volatile`, `__unaligned` qualifiers.

### static_cast

Compile‑time checked conversions (numeric, pointer up/down, constructors).

### reinterpret_cast

Low‑level bit reinterpretation (pointer ↔ integer, unrelated types).

### dynamic_cast

Runtime‑checked safe cast for polymorphic types.
